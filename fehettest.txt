Modified Wald Test Add-in for EViews
Version: 1.1
Author: Hoang Ba Manh
Date: 06-Jan-2026
Support: hbmanh9492@gmail.com

Description:
This add-in implements the Modified-Wald and Kezdi test 
	for heteroskedasticity in fixed-effects models.

1. Modified-Wald test by Greene (2000)

Hypotheses:
- H0: Groupwise Homoskedasticity.
- H1: Groupwise heteroskedasticity.

Requirements:
- Large T panel data.
- Cross-section Fixed-effects.

Limitations:
- Not appropriate for small-T panels.


2. LM test by Kezdi (2003)

Null hypothesis:
- H1: Cross-sectional homoskedasticity.
- H2: Serially uncorrelated: e_it, x_it or both.
- H3: Homoskedasticity and serially uncorrelated.

Alternative hypothesis
- Ha: System heteroskedasticity.

Requirements:
- Large N, small T panel data.
- Cross-section Fixed-effects.

Limitations:
- Not appropriate for small-N panels.

Usage:
Select the Equation object, then run phettest from the add-in menu
